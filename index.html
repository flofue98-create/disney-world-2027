Lightning Lane

Magic Kingdom — First Day

Purchase Lightning Lane Multi Pass. If the current system continues, purchase separate access for Seven Dwarfs Mine Train. Peter Pan’s Flight is the preferred advance Multi Pass priority.

Disney’s Hollywood Studios — First Day

Purchase Lightning Lane Multi Pass. Prioritize Slinky Dog Dash and protect Mickey & Minnie’s Runaway Railway. Separate paid access for Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance is a strong consideration if the 2027 system remains similar.

Disney’s Animal Kingdom

Do not purchase Lightning Lane Multi Pass by default. Reconsider if Tropical Americas opens, waits rise materially, or the 2027 attraction lineup changes.

EPCOT

Do not purchase Lightning Lane Multi Pass by default. Use the Beach Club and International Gateway Early Entry advantage for Remy’s Ratatouille Adventure.

Magic Kingdom — Second Day

Do not purchase by default. Add it only if the first Magic Kingdom day leaves several important attractions unfinished.

Disney’s Hollywood Studios — Second Day

Do not purchase by default. Add it only for major missed attractions, operational problems or a major demand change caused by Monstropolis.

Family Touring Notes

On the family’s first entrance to each park, everyone begins together. Adult ride splits happen later.
Nonna avoids thrill rides, roller coasters and tight enclosed spaces. For theaters and shows, plan for an aisle or end seat whenever possible.
Mike is uncomfortable with heights. Height-based attractions are never treated as mandatory for him.
Liliana’s height and comfort with darkness and drops will be reassessed closer to the trip.
During adult-only attractions, Nonna and Liliana get a nearby meaningful activity rather than simply waiting.
Angelo is vegetarian and occasionally eats fish. Restaurant choices need a real vegetarian option.
